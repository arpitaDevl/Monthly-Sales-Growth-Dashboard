# Monthly Sales Growth Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive Power BI dashboard that tracks monthly sales performance, revenue trends, and business growth through dynamic visualizations and key performance indicators (KPIs).

## Project Overview

The Monthly Sales Growth Dashboard provides a clear view of sales performance across different months, helping businesses monitor revenue trends, compare monthly performance, and identify growth opportunities. The dashboard is designed with interactive filters and KPI cards for quick and effective analysis.

## Dashboard Features

- Total Sales KPI
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Month-over-Month (MoM) Growth Analysis
- Revenue Performance Overview
- Interactive Slicers and Filters
- Dynamic KPI Cards
- User-friendly Dashboard Layout

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Total Sales | Overall sales generated |
| Monthly Revenue | Revenue earned each month |
| MoM Growth | Percentage growth compared to the previous month |
| Sales Trend | Monthly performance visualization |
